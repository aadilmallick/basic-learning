Subject: Your AI App Shipping Checklist

> Here is your AI App Shipping Checklist: [resource link].
>
> Start with one small feature. Mark each item Yes, No, or Unknown, then choose one concrete next action.

## E02 A valid response can still be wrong

Send condition: Two days later, only for people who consented to teaching and promotional email.

Subject: A valid response can still be wrong

> The brief has every field your application expected. That is useful, but it does not establish that the client requested everything inside it.
>
> Try this fictional note: “We might need customer accounts later.”
>
> If your generated brief says “Customer accounts are included,” the application has turned uncertainty into scope.
>
> For this workflow, I want stated requirements, assumptions, and open questions represented separately. I also want a person to review the draft.
>
> Your task today: write one input where a complete-looking answer would be misleading. State what acceptable behavior would look like before you run it.
>
> The checklist is here if you need it: [resource link].
>
> Aadil
