## 7. Email drafts

These are editable structures. Add the real links, required sender/footer information, appropriate opt-out handling, and accurate subscription context. Review the actual rules and provider behavior before sending.

### A. Deliver the requested sample

**Subject:** Your [topic] sample

Here is [the resource] you requested: [link]. Start with [specific first step]. The example assumes [prerequisites].

You will also receive [accurate description of the messages the person agreed to receive]. If something in the sample is unclear, you can reply with the point where you got stuck.

[Sender and applicable footer.]

### B. Teach one useful point

**Subject:** A useful way to approach [specific problem]

In the sample, [describe the concrete case]. The important step is [mechanism], because [reason]. You can inspect it by [small action or worked comparison].

The complete explanation is here: [relevant resource link].

[Sender and applicable footer.]

### C. Explain the full learning path

**Subject:** How the [product] examples connect

The sample covers [bounded task]. The full guide continues through [actual sequence], so you can [supported outcome]. It assumes [prerequisites] and does not include [important exclusion].

You can inspect the contents and terms here: [sales-page link].

[Sender and applicable footer.]

### D. Make the offer

**Subject:** [Product] is available

I created [product] for [specific audience and situation]. It contains [actual assets] and uses [teaching mechanism].

The price is [amount and currency; explain applicable display as needed]. Access arrives [actual process and timing]. The page explains [support, update, and refund information].

Read the sample: [link]. View the full offer: [link].

If the prerequisites or format do not fit what you need, use the sample to decide before buying.

[Sender and applicable footer.]

### E. Buyer onboarding

**Subject:** Start here: [purchased product]

Your access is here: [secure appropriate route]. Begin with [orientation or first useful task]. Check [prerequisites/setup] before starting the labs.

If access fails, contact [actual support route]. Your purchase includes [accurate support scope].

[Appropriate transactional information. Do not assume this message authorizes unrelated marketing.]

### F. Product feedback

**Subject:** Was the first [lab/chapter] clear?

Did access work, and was there a point where the first example became difficult to follow? A short reply about the step is useful. Feedback is optional and can be critical.

[If requesting public use of a statement, ask separately and preserve context.]

## 8. Collaboration proposal draft

I teach [specific subject] for [audience]. Your coverage of [actual relevant work you inspected] seems connected to [shared problem]. I can contribute [bounded useful session/article/demo] that helps people [task].

If that fits your audience, I would like to discuss [format, expectations, and terms]. The sample of my teaching is [link]. Any product promotion or compensation would be agreed and disclosed appropriately.

Personalize this with facts. Do not pretend to have read a partner's work when you have not.
