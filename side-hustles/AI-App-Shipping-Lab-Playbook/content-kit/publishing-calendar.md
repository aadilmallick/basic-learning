# First month execution calendar

# 8 The first 30 days from zero

This is a sequence of workdays and publishing days relative to your start, not a forecast that followers or sales arrive on schedule. Day 1 is the day you begin. Move the enrollment window if the reference project or teaching materials are not ready.

The default primary channels are LinkedIn, your own writing, and email. Publish two YouTube videos in the month. X and TikTok use adapted excerpts; they are optional distribution tests, not five independent production commitments.

## Your zero-audience acquisition work

List 30 people or introduction paths with a real reason for fit. Include developer contacts, relevant alumni groups, project collaborators, meetup organizers, and people who have publicly discussed the problem.

Seek 15 short conversations. Make the paid offer to at least ten genuinely qualified people after explaining the project. A proposed gate is five paid starts. Do not convert interview compliments into demand.

If you have five appropriate buyers before the workshop, deliver the small pilot and keep the workshop as a later acquisition test. If the workshop has few attendees, teach it usefully, record it with permission, and learn from the questions. Do not fake a crowd.

## What counts as progress

A working funnel is progress. A useful conversation is progress. A purchase by an appropriate learner is stronger evidence. A completed project and renewal are stronger still.

The calendar organizes execution. The evidence determines whether you expand.
