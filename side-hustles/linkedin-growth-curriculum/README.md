# LinkedIn Growth, Digital Marketing, and Short-Form Video for Developers

This course is a practical, first-principles program for Aadil Mallick: a full-stack developer who wants to build an audience, teach technical ideas, demonstrate useful software, and launch MicroSaaS products without sounding manufactured.

## The outcome

By the end, you will be able to:

- position a developer brand around a clear audience and value promise;
- choose a niche without trapping yourself in a single topic;
- reverse-engineer successful creators without plagiarizing them;
- write hooks, stories, tutorials, build-in-public updates, and launch posts;
- plan, record, edit, caption, and publish LinkedIn-native short videos;
- create screen-recorded software demonstrations that remain readable on a phone;
- connect content to a digital-marketing funnel for a MicroSaaS;
- use LinkedIn analytics to run disciplined experiments rather than chase vanity metrics;
- execute a repeatable 90-day publishing and launch system.

## How to study

Read lessons in order the first time. Each lesson begins with the problem, builds a mental model, demonstrates it with developer-focused examples, and ends with an application. The application work is optional, but the course only becomes useful when you publish. Do not wait until Lesson 8 to post: publish one small experiment after each lesson.

Use a real project as the recurring case study. The suggested starting project is your math whiteboard with native LaTeX and graphing because it has an obvious visual payoff, a defined audience, and both technical and product stories. Your screen recorder, YouTube timestamp tool, API-key manager, and future MicroSaaS projects can then become follow-up series.

## Important operating assumptions

- The goal is relevant attention, trust, and product demand—not maximum impressions at any cost.
- “Virality” is a probabilistic outcome. You can improve the inputs but cannot guarantee distribution.
- LinkedIn changes. Platform-specific facts in this course were checked on November 13, 2026; verify current upload limits and features before relying on them.
- The supplied shortened LinkedIn creator URL could not be resolved in the available research environment. Lesson 3 includes a complete method for adding that creator once the profile name or accessible URL is available; no traits are fabricated.
- Creator examples are analyzed as public patterns, not as permission to reproduce their wording, assets, or identity.

## Your course thesis

Your defensible creator position is:

> I build practical AI and web products, show how they work, and explain the engineering and product decisions behind shipping them.

The recommended content blend is:

- **Visual discovery:** fast, compelling demos of tools and technologies.
- **Technical depth:** useful explanations that prove you understand what you show.
- **Builder narrative:** honest stories about decisions, mistakes, users, and iteration.
- **Commercial intent:** occasional, transparent paths from useful content to a product, waitlist, or conversation.

That combination borrows useful mechanics from successful creators while remaining grounded in work only you can show.
