## Hook generator

Write one of each:

1. Result: “I built/tested…”
2. Problem: “I kept…”
3. Contrast: “The prototype…, but production…”
4. Counterintuitive: “The important part is not…”
5. Mistake: “I built/chose… before…”
6. Identity: “If you are…”
7. Experiment: “I compared/tested…”
8. Story: “A user asked…”

Select the hook whose promise the body can best prove.

## Mobile and privacy QA

- First frame explains the category and value.
- Critical text is readable on a phone.
- Captions are accurate and inside safe margins.
- Cursor movement is slow and purposeful.
- No email, chat, notification, bookmark, token, customer, internal URL, or personal data appears.
- Dummy accounts and synthetic data are used.
- Source builders are credited.
- Sponsorship/affiliate/free access is disclosed clearly.
- Export was watched once muted and once with headphones.

## 30-minute weekly review

1. Update mature metrics.
2. Mark the intended job of each post.
3. Compare results with the intended metric.
4. Read and classify comments/DMs.
5. Separate topic, packaging, and substance issues.
6. Select one variable to test.
7. Add audience questions to the idea bank.
8. Confirm next week’s three pieces and recording buffer.
