# Verification report

**Edition date:** November 13, 2026.

## Deliverable scope

The course contains 12 full teaching lessons, five editable workbook documents, a glossary, a source register, and a reading guide. It also includes a self-contained HTML edition for offline reading. Lessons teach concepts through explanations, recurring examples, tradeoffs, failure modes, and optional applications rather than only listing topics.

## Content and source review

The prerequisite sequence runs from buyer problems and business fundamentals through offer design, communication, distribution, measurement, launches, and customer success. The fictional product and numerical cases are labeled as instructional assumptions. Platform-specific and introductory U.S. compliance claims have linked primary sources with an access date. No claim is made to have accessed private creator analytics or to have run a campaign.

Examples of incomplete sales copy in templates are intentional, labeled drafting fields. They must be filled in and checked against the user's actual product before publication. The course does not contain an operating storefront or configured email system.

## Mechanical checks

The included verification-results.json records lesson word counts, Markdown fence balance, local-link and anchor checks, required document checks, and verified arithmetic for representative worked examples. Counts use Python whitespace-separated words and are not token counts. No per-lesson token minimum was specified for this request; exact model-token length was not measured.

The archive is checked for integrity and expected contents. The offline HTML edition is statically checked for matching navigation targets, unique element IDs, and absence of required external assets. Its inline JavaScript is syntax-checked and its filtering logic is exercised with a simulated document. These are not rendered-browser tests. A browser verification was attempted, but this environment blocked Chromium startup; visual layout, actual browser interactions, and mobile rendering remain unverified. The machine-readable report preserves that limitation.

## What these checks do not establish

The course's business examples are calculations and instructional cases, not observed conversion benchmarks. No market demand, future revenue, product-market fit, advertising profitability, legal compliance of an unseen business, email delivery, payment transaction, or customer learning outcome has been established. Those require the user's actual implementation and evidence.

The course intentionally avoids fixed vendor prices and claims about guaranteed distribution. Recheck current official documentation and relevant business obligations when implementing.
