# Research Notes and Sources

Access date: **November 13, 2026**.

## Primary LinkedIn guidance

1. [LinkedIn Create: a guide to creating engaging posts](https://members.linkedin.com/create-start) — LinkedIn recommends content grounded in expertise, personal lessons, industry news, behind-the-scenes work, thought leadership, and success stories.
2. [Share videos on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a7494039) — upload flow, captions, thumbnails, safe zones, and current technical limits. At access time: 75 KB–5 GB, 2–3 second minimum depending on device, 15-minute maximum, 256×144 through 4096×2304 resolution, 1:2.4 through 2.4:1 aspect ratio, and 10–60 fps.
3. [Auto captions for videos](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1327025) — generating, reviewing, editing, and removing captions.
4. [Post analytics](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a518885) — impressions, members reached, network split, profile viewers, followers gained, reactions, comments, reposts, saves, sends, link visits, viewer demographics, video views, total watch time, and average watch time. LinkedIn describes analytics as estimates.
5. [Creator analytics](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a704175) — combined post trends, audience growth and demographics, top posts, date ranges, and spreadsheet export.
6. [Analytics and tools](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1432215) — current location of content, audience, newsletter, and profile analytics.
7. [Updates to Creator Mode](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a5999182) — the old Creator Mode toggle was removed; creator tools and analytics remain available.
8. [LinkedIn creator insights](https://members.linkedin.com/create-insights) — current posting, format, engagement, and growth guidance.
9. [LinkedIn B2B video guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/content-marketing/13-top-tips-for-compelling-b2b-video-content-on-linkedin) — video growth, differentiation, and B2B use cases.
10. [LinkedIn 2026 executive video guide](https://business.linkedin.com/content/dam/lem/business/en/advertise/agencies/pdfs/Turning_Agency_Executive_Presence_into_Engagement_2026.pdf) — episodic formats, early problem framing, captions, native upload, clear audio, comments, and follow-up videos.
11. [LinkedIn guidance for AI/search visibility](https://www.linkedin.com/business/marketing/blog/content-marketing/how-to-leverage-linkedin-for-ai-visibility-in-2026) — originality, subject-matter credibility, descriptive openings, depth, and consistent publishing. Treat performance numbers in marketing material as directional, not universal guarantees.

## Creator observations

### Ram Maheshwari / rammcodes

- [Public LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rammcodes)
- Research snapshot showed roughly 188,000 followers and more than 3,000 posts.
- Repeated observable patterns: high frequency; timely AI, coding, web-design, and developer-tool discoveries; an emotionally expressive opening; a rapidly understandable benefit; short feature lists; a source link; and a consistent follow prompt.
- Some posts are curated discoveries or reposts, not original builds. That matters: the transferable skill is discovery and packaging, while Aadil’s advantage is adding first-hand testing and engineering judgment.

### Fatimah Hussain

- [Public LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fatimah-hussain)
- Research snapshot showed roughly 22,000 LinkedIn followers. A public post states that her broader educational brand reached 250,000 followers, 200 million views, and 50 million accounts in under a year; those are creator-stated figures, not independently audited here.
- Repeated observable patterns: an explicit student audience; practical education; first-person stories; approachable, informal language; measurable milestones; product-building connected to audience needs; partnerships; event/community proof; and invitations that feel native to her journey.

## Ethics and disclosure

- [FTC Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers) — disclose material relationships clearly and conspicuously; do not bury a disclosure after “see more” or rely on vague wording. For video endorsements, put disclosure in the video as well as the post where appropriate.

## Evidence limits

Public LinkedIn activity pages can be partial, personalized, reordered, or inaccessible without sign-in. The creator analysis is a qualitative pattern study from accessible profile/activity samples, not a complete statistical audit of every post. Follower counts and features are snapshots and will change.
