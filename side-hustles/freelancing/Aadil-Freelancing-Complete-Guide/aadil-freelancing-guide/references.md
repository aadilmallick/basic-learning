# Research references and evidence limits

Research checked **November 13, 2026**. Links below are primary sources: your public pages, provider documentation, official company pages, and US government guidance. A provider page establishes its own features or terms; it does not establish independent effectiveness, freelancer adoption, expected income, or the presence of an open contract. Recheck prices, eligibility, permitted use, and actual checkout terms before committing money or promising a client a deployment route.

Personalization also uses the two supplied project files: **Aadil-Mallick-Project-Context.md** and **Aadil_s_Resume_SWE_variant.pdf**. The guide does not redistribute either source file. Resume achievements are attributed, not independently audited; current employment, client permissions, available hours, and income goals remain unconfirmed. Historical extension ratings differ across the supplied sources, so no current rating is used in the recommendation.

## What this research does not establish

No prospects were contacted, no purchasing intent was confirmed, and no open role is implied by the seed list. No independent market-rate survey or freelancer tool-adoption survey was performed. Prices, channel allocations, capacity estimates, and revenue examples are labeled strategy experiments or hypothetical arithmetic. No client extension, application, production security review, store submission, legal contract review, or payment integration was executed. The source log remains a working research artifact; the user-facing references here record the relevant findings and limitations without redistributing third-party pages.
